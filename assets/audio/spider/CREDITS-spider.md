# Spider-Man (Iron Spider) sound bank -- credits

Every `sp_*` sound in this folder is built by `build_audio.py` (same folder) from the real recordings
listed below: cut sample-accurately, varispeeded (tape-style resampling, incl. pitch glides), reversed,
enveloped, EQ'd (incl. a time-varying resonant band swept through recorded noise), compressed and
layered. No oscillator synthesis anywhere. `python3 build_audio.py --fetch && python3 build_audio.py`
downloads the sources (re-checking each licence on the source site) and rebuilds the whole bank.

## How each sound was built

* **`sp_thwip_1`** -- layers: [1] finger snap (Finger clicks) cut to its first ~30 ms, HP 300 Hz; [2] pump-spray "pfft" burst, HP 2.5 kHz, 1 ms attack, ~25 ms decay, varispeed 1.1->0.92; [3a] the same air + a fishing-rod swish (Freesound 371313) through a resonant band gliding 6.5 -> 1.3 kHz; [3b] Shhhwwshhhw air-whistle played with a falling varispeed (2.4x -> 1.0x) = the descending zip; [4] rope-zip (Freesound 527421) sped 2-4x with falling rate; [5] the click slowed and LP 450 Hz for body; bus HP 110 Hz + 2.5:1 compression.  _Sources: Pumpspray pfft, Fishing Rod Swish Swoosh.wav, ROPEMvmt_Rope Zip Slow_MP_9.wav, Shhhwwshhhw, Finger clicks. Level: short-window K-loudness -15.0 (true-peak capped)._
* **`sp_thwip_2`** -- layers: [1] bull-whip crack tip (OGA whip.wav) cut to its first ~30 ms, HP 300 Hz; [2] air-duster can burst, HP 2.5 kHz, 1 ms attack, ~25 ms decay, varispeed 1.1->0.92; [3a] the same air + a fishing-rod swish (Freesound 371313) through a resonant band gliding 7.5 -> 1.7 kHz; [3b] pen whoosh (Freesound 391441) air-whistle played with a falling varispeed (1.9x -> 0.85x) = the descending zip; [4] rope-zip (Freesound 527421) sped 2-4x with falling rate; [5] the click slowed and LP 450 Hz for body; bus HP 110 Hz + 2.5:1 compression. Whole shot varispeeded x1.08; body from the air-nailer shot.  _Sources: Air duster short, air_nailer_single_shot_1.wav, Pen whoosh 1.wav, Fishing Rod Swish Swoosh.wav, ROPEMvmt_Rope Zip Slow_MP_9.wav, Whip sound. Level: short-window K-loudness -16.0 (true-peak capped)._
* **`sp_thwip_3`** -- layers: [1] snapping twig (pitched x1.2) cut to its first ~30 ms, HP 300 Hz; [2] pneumatic air-nailer exhaust (Freesound 131304) burst, HP 2.5 kHz, 1 ms attack, ~25 ms decay, varispeed 1.1->0.92; [3a] the same air + a fishing-rod swish (Freesound 371313) through a resonant band gliding 5.6 -> 1.15 kHz; [3b] Shhhwwshhhw air-whistle played with a falling varispeed (2.1x -> 0.85x) = the descending zip; [4] rope-zip (Freesound 527421) sped 2-4x with falling rate; [5] the click slowed and LP 450 Hz for body; bus HP 110 Hz + 2.5:1 compression. Whole shot varispeeded x0.93.  _Sources: air_nailer_single_shot_1.wav, Fishing Rod Swish Swoosh.wav, ROPEMvmt_Rope Zip Slow_MP_9.wav, Shhhwwshhhw, Finger clicks, Snapping twig (single). Level: short-window K-loudness -15.6 (true-peak capped)._
* **`sp_thwip_4`** -- layers: [1] air-rifle shot (Freesound 466310) cut to its first ~30 ms, HP 300 Hz; [2] deodorant spray burst, HP 2.5 kHz, 1 ms attack, ~25 ms decay, varispeed 1.1->0.92; [3a] the same air + a fishing-rod swish (Freesound 371313) through a resonant band gliding 7 -> 1.5 kHz; [3b] Shhhwwshhhw air-whistle played with a falling varispeed (2.7x -> 1.15x) = the descending zip; [4] rope-zip (Freesound 527421) sped 2-4x with falling rate; [5] the click slowed and LP 450 Hz for body; bus HP 110 Hz + 2.5:1 compression. Whole shot varispeeded x1.03.  _Sources: Deodorant spray (short), Air Rifle, Fishing Rod Swish Swoosh.wav, ROPEMvmt_Rope Zip Slow_MP_9.wav, Shhhwwshhhw, Finger clicks. ## Loudness

* Sounds >= 0.4 s: gain set from ffmpeg `loudnorm` (EBU R128) measurement to **-16 LUFS integrated**,
  applied as a single linear gain (no dynamic processing), capped so the true peak stays <= -2 dBTP.
* One-shots < 0.4 s: integrated loudness is not meaningful that short, so the gain is set from the
  loudest 100 ms K-weighted window (target -11.0 LUFS on that window, which is where the -16 LUFS-I
  items land), with the same true-peak cap.
* `gain` in index.json balances ids against each other (sp_thwip_1 = 1.0 reference).
* Loops: use the `.ogg` in engine (MP3 encoders add padding at the start, which breaks gapless looping).
