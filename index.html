Iron Man

Suit up.
Then fly.

Mark I to Mark 85, each with its own suit-up: the villa gantry, the Mark 42 flying to you piece by piece, nanotech pouring over Tony. Then six degrees of freedom and nothing between you and the skyline.

Spider-Man

Every line
is real.

Webs anchor to real buildings, the rope pulls taut, and you let go at the top of the arc. Crawl up the glass, zip to a ledge, drop between the towers.

Requirements

  • A desktop or laptop — Windows, macOS or Linux
  • Chrome or Edge, up to date (WebGL 2)
  • A graphics card like an RTX 3050 or better
  • A controller — DualShock 4 / DualSense or Xbox, USB or Bluetooth
  • About 300 MB downloads on the first start, then it's cached

Start playing

  1. Plug in or pair a controller.
  2. Press Play. The city streams in — the bar shows the progress.
  3. Press ✕ / A, pick Iron Man or Spider-Man, choose Solo.
  4. Press F11 for full screen.

Controls

  • L R fly, run, look
  • ✕ climb / jump · ○ descend / dodge
  • L1 R1 repulsors · web shots · R2 boost · swing
  • PAD city map · OPTIONS pause
  • Keyboard works in the menus (arrows, Enter, Esc) — flying and swinging need a controller
